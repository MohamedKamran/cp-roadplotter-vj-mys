Camera settings

Height and tilt turn each detection into a real pothole location. Measure them once for your mount. Field of view is the horizontal angle the camera sees (most phones: 60–75° in landscape). Bigger frames catch smaller potholes but use more data: 1280 px at 5 fps is roughly 5–8 Mbps.
